    Attention Atlas is a public, spatial record of what Tanisha Raghav is finding, making, and paying attention to on the internet. It is not a feed or a folder of bookmarks. It is a field you can move through, where links, images, and notes sit according to her relationship with them rather than by date or topic.

    Two loose axes orient the map. Along “found it – made it” sit things encountered online and things made in response. Along “passing by – can’t stop thinking about it” sit fleeting discoveries and the threads she keeps returning to. Placement is a personal gesture, not a classification: an item lives where it feels right, and it can move as attention shifts.

    The atlas is unfinished on purpose. It is meant to show attention as it is: partial, wandering, and occasionally obsessive.
